Reach Up for the Sky

An immersive 3D environment exploring how biased AI-generated content can influence a girl’s ambitions.

Tech: Unity, Blender, Clip Studio Paint, Photoshop, ChatGPT

01. Visualising Infinite Potential

To represent the expansive imagination of childhood, I developed a sketched aesthetic that combines 2D hand-drawn assets with a blank 3D white space.

The experience is structured as a vertical tower where each floor represents a discovered interest. Vibrant imagery and particle effects were utilised to romanticize various career paths, mirroring early age ambition before it meets the gendered biases amplified by AI.

02. A Weighted Reality

The ascent is a subversion. Upon reaching the final floor, the player’s expectation of “the top” is overturned as they emerge at a city ground level. Despite the efforts to climb and dream, girls are still restricted by structures above.

The city utilizes a grayscale palette and repetitive blocks to evoke a sense of stagnation. A cube mapped sky of AI generated women in stereotypical fields boxes the user within a ceiling of algorithm bias, making the sky limiting.
