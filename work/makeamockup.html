Make a Mockup!

A browser-based game that simplifies the early stages of fashion design.

Tech: Illustrator, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Drag Drop and Design

The core of the game is a simple drag and drop system that is used to mix and match sections of clothing. Players can move different bodice and skirt pieces onto a central mannequin, letting them find their favourite combinations.

Boutique Aesthetics

The interface is inspired by parisian vintage storefronts. The bold black stripes and scallops creates charm while also separating the menus of bodices and skirts, making it easier for players to see their options and create focus on the central mannequin. White with black outlines were used for the piece illustrations to mimic the flat images found in pattern drafting books, emphasizing the mockup experience.
