Elsewhere

An immersive 3D environment depicting the transition from social misalignment to the peace of solitude.

Tech: Unity, Blender, BandLab, C#

Social Submersion

The start is a disorienting party. Through custom Voronoi shaders, with fisheye lens and reverb heavy audio, it mimics the "drunken" blur of a social gathering. These layers create a space that is overwhelming, manifesting the weight of social misalignment.

The environment is intentionally difficult to navigate, featuring narrow, squishy pathways and bubbles that obstruct movement. The interaction of finding and popping balloons in this space depicts disengagement; once enough are popped, the world turns anxious, reflecting the fear that the player's presence has inadvertently burst the bubbly atmosphere for others.

Path to Solitude

This transition encourages a retreat from that environment. Walking out of this space opens into a natural, serene landscape representing a state of quiet liberation. The experience concludes at a secluded resting point, offering a perspective to look back at the vibrant lives others have bloomed in, allowing for appreciation from a distance.
