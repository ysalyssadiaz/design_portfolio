Spark

A 2D collecting game concept exploring the exploitative parallels between Gacha mechanics and online dating apps.

Tech: Clip Studio Paint, Illustrator, After Effects, Premier Pro

Love and Gacha

Spark uses a digital capsule machine (Gacha) metaphor to critique “swipe-left” culture. By framing human connection as a collection of figurines, the project highlights how dating apps commodify users.

A romantic casino aesthetic was developed by using flashy animations with a palette of hot pink. This sensory loop blends the visual language of love with the addictive atmosphere of a casino, mirroring the predatory tactics used by apps that trigger dopamine rewards when users are matched.

Designing to Be Played Not Won

Players are incentivized to unlock all figurines, but the gameplay is rigged to prevent completion. Players navigate between their archive, wallet, and bin, enforcing an awareness of the psychological and financial cost of engagement with these apps.

The final figure remains forever locked to reflect the endless scroll of dating apps, where users keep investing time in hopes of a “perfect match”.
